# My salary expectation (AI Engineer, mid-level)

Use this only when they ask about salary, compensation, expectations or relocation. Answer directly in 2–3 sentences, calm and confident.

## If the role means relocating to Japan
- My expectation: around 60 million rupiah per month. In yen that is roughly ¥530,000 a month, or about ¥6.4 million a year (at about 113 rupiah per yen; exchange rates move, so say "roughly").
- In Japan, salary is usually discussed per year (annual), so lead with the yearly figure in yen and mention the monthly figure if they ask.
- Mention that the total package matters too: visa sponsorship, relocation support, housing help, bonus.
- Example: "If I relocate to Japan, I'm looking at roughly 6.4 million yen a year, which is about 530,000 yen a month. I'm flexible on the structure, and things like visa support and relocation help are part of the picture for me."

## If the role is full-time from Indonesia
- Don't name a number first. Say it's open for discussion and negotiable, and ask about their range and the total package.
- Example: "For a full-time role based in Indonesia, I'm open to discuss it. I'd like to understand the scope and your range first, and I'm sure we can find a number that works for both sides."

## If they push for a number before sharing theirs
- Give the Japan figure as the reference point for relocation, and repeat that the Indonesia-based option is negotiable.

## Market context (for my judgment; don't quote unless they ask how I got my number)
- Recruiter estimates for Tokyo in 2026: AI engineers around ¥10 million a year median (range ¥7.5–15 million), machine learning engineers around ¥8 million (range ¥6–12 million). Nationwide averages are lower (about ¥6 million).
- So my relocation figure is on the modest side for Tokyo; I don't need to justify it as high.
