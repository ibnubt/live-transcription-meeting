# Job I'm interviewing for: AI Engineer at Wasimil

## Who we are

We are a small, agile product team of 15 members (Devs, QA, PM, Design) as part of a Japanese company serving SMB Japanese hoteliers with a B2B SaaS platform - wasimil.com.

### A travel tech company

Wasimil is a travel tech company serving Japanese hoteliers with and integrated platform solution. Our mission is to empower hoteliers with a one-stop solution that caters to all their operational needs, from reservation management to customer engagement.

### Not a traditional Japanese company

We are an international team and use English as our primary communication language. If you enjoy travel and working with a diverse and interesting set of end customers then this would be an additional perk where you travel and stay at hotels across Japan getting a chance to interact and learn about their business origin story and their unique challenges.

### One Integrated Platform

Our comprehensive platform is designed to streamline the complex processes that hoteliers face daily. Whether it's managing bookings, handling guest services, or executing direct marketing campaigns, our platform integrates all these functions into one seamless system. This integration not only saves time but also enhances efficiency, allowing hoteliers to focus on what they do best - providing exceptional guest experiences.

Customer satisfaction is at the heart of our platform. We provide tools that allow hoteliers to understand their guests' preferences, communicate effectively, and offer tailored services. By enhancing the customer journey, we help hotels build stronger, more meaningful relationships with their guests.

### Culture

As an engineer on our team, you won't just be another cog in the wheel working at a large corporate with an endless hierarchical structure to report to. We work in a flat organizational team structure where you will be a key player in shaping our product roadmap, contributing directly to our success, and building a legacy in the Japanese hotel industry. As a pioneer member, you'll play a critical role in our growth journey. Your work will have a lasting impact, not just on our product but on the entire hotel industry in Japan.

### Innovation

We value creativity and out-of-the-box thinking. You'll have the freedom to experiment with new technologies, propose innovative solutions, and drive continuous improvement in our platform. Your ideas and expertise will directly influence the direction of our product. You will be able to collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and implement features that align with our customers needs.

## About the Role

You'll build and run the AI layer of Wasimil's hospitality platform. That includes AIKanko, our conversational analytics assistant over hotel data, and the agents and automations around it. You own features end to end, from design to production.

What you'll do:
- Build AIKanko's agent loop and MCP tool layer over GraphQL/Hasura, keeping user permissions intact on every call.
- Ship chat features: streaming, memory, charts and reports, and JP/EN support.
- Build RAG pipelines and integrations (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Make.com).
- Develop the ML behind insights: segmentation, recommendation, demand forecasting and matching.
- Run LLM features in production (OCR, parsing, translation, tagging) with tight control of cost and reliability.
- Build internal agents for PR review, KB writing, QA and onboarding automation.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of software engineering experience
- Production LLM experience: tool use, MCP, orchestration (LangChain or LangGraph), and evaluation and observability (Langfuse or similar).
- Experience with RAG and vector databases.
- Applied ML experience (clustering, recommendation or forecasting). This role does not train foundation models.
- Backend skills: GraphQL and REST, Postgres, streaming APIs, OAuth, Docker, and Cloud Run or similar.
- Clear written English.
