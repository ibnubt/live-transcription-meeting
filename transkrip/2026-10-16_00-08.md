[00:08:53] Mereka: Walk me through how you would design a flash sales system.
           ↳ Saran (Sonnet 5.5): Let me start with assumptions: 10,000 items, 100,000 users in the first seconds, and no overselling.  The product page comes from a CDN and cache.
